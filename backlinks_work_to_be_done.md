## 🚀 How Google Crawls & Counts Backlinks Automatically
Even if client site owners do not perform SEO or use Google Search Console:
1. **Automated Googlebot Crawling:** Google's web crawlers continuously visit and re-crawl public live domains on Vercel and custom domains.
2. **Link Discovery:** Once the `<a href="https://chittortech.online">` code is deployed live, Googlebot detects the link during its routine crawl and attributes link equity to ChittorTech.
3. **Domain Authority Boost:** Each indexed client website acts as an active external backlink, increasing ChittorTech's Google Search ranking and lead volume across India.
