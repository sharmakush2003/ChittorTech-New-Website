# Manual SEO Addition & Indexing Guide

This guide outlines the steps to manually submit the new Google Play Publishing service page to Google Search Console and optimize its visibility. Since Play Store publishing and testing services are rare in India, proper indexing will capture high-intent organic leads.

---

## 🚀 1. Google Search Console Manual Submission

Follow these steps to request immediate indexing from Google:

1. **Open Google Search Console**: Log in to your [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
2. **Inspect the URL**: Copy the full link of the new page:
   `https://chittortech.online/google-play-publishing`
3. **Paste in URL Inspection Tool**: Paste this URL in the search bar at the top of Search Console and press Enter.
4. **Request Indexing**: Click the **"Request Indexing"** button. Google will test if the page is live and add it to its priority crawl queue.

---

## 🗺️ 2. Sitemap Update Submission

Since we updated `sitemap.xml`, you should ping Google to fetch the sitemap again:

1. Navigate to **Sitemaps** on the left menu of Search Console.
2. In the **"Add a new sitemap"** section, enter:
   `sitemap.xml`
3. Click **Submit**. This triggers Google to crawl the newly added `<url>` node for `/google-play-publishing`.
